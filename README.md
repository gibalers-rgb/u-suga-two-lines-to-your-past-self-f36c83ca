## The entry

> Don't leave the university letter unopened in the drawer the night Dad
> comes home carrying his toolbox and no job.
>
> You'll find it still sealed three years later, and the degree inside was
> the one way out of the coffee shop where you still work at thirty.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes the two lines to /output/entry.txt
```

`main.ts` is a fixed, deterministic program: no network, no randomness, no
input files. It validates the bounty shape before writing — exactly two
lines, one sentence each, 1–280 characters — then writes
`/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this text

- **One specific moment:** the night Dad comes home with his toolbox and
  no job, and the letter left unopened in the drawer.
- **Concrete enough to picture:** a drawer, a toolbox, a sealed letter
  found three years later, a coffee shop at thirty.
- **Real cause and effect:** the unopened letter is the degree never used,
  which is why the writer is still in the coffee shop — the second line
  traces the consequence instead of restating the warning.
- **Plain voice:** written to one person, not an audience, with no wasted
  words.
